# 🚨 Pulse On Point

### AI-Powered Emergency Response & Rescue Coordination Platform

Pulse On Point is an intelligent emergency response platform designed to reduce response times during road accidents, medical emergencies, fires, and other critical situations. The platform combines real-time GPS tracking, voice activation, emergency coordination, multilingual assistance, and live responder monitoring to provide immediate help when every second matters.

---

## 🌟 Problem Statement

Emergency situations often suffer from:

* Delayed reporting
* Lack of location awareness
* Panic among bystanders
* Communication barriers
* Slow emergency coordination
* Limited access to real-time rescue information

These delays can significantly impact survival rates during the critical "Golden Hour."

Pulse On Point aims to bridge this gap by providing a smart, automated, and user-friendly emergency response system.
